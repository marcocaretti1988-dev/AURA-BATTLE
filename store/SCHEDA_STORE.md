## Full description – EN
Welcome to AURA BATTLE, the arcade dance battle where aura is everything.

Pick your dancer, step into the piazza and challenge rivals move after move: hit the buttons on the beat as notes enter the circle, chain combos, fill your aura bar and unleash the SPECIAL MOVE in front of a wild crowd.

• TOUR OF ITALY – 6 piazzas: Milan Cathedral, Spanish Steps, Naples Plebiscito, the Colosseum, Bologna Piazza Maggiore and Palermo Piazza della Vergogna, each with its own song
• 6 CHARACTERS with their own dances and aura
• FREE STYLE – endless dancing, the lane keeps speeding up
• AURA CHALLENGE – repeat longer and longer sequences with 3 lives
• Gold notes, double notes and hold notes
• 3D crowd that dances, cheers and whistles, announcer, shooting stars and light effects
• Tutorial, audio calibration, Italian and English
• Unlock characters and piazzas, beat your records and share your result

No account, no ads, no data collected. Just you, the music and the piazza.
